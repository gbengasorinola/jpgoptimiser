How to Compress

  1. Upload the files you want to shrink.
  2. Open the Compress tab and set your target size.
  3. Click Process Files to generate smaller outputs.

How to Resize

  1. Select the Resize tab after uploading your files.
  2. Enter the width and height you want.
  3. Choose a background mode and process the files.

How to Stamp Logo

  1. Open the Stamp Logo tab.
  2. Upload your logo image and pick a placement.
  3. Process the files to add the logo to each asset.
